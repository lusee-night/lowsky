# Radio loops and Local Bubble: bounded observational constraint pass

As of 2026-10-13. `measurements.json` contains transcribed, source-located numbers; `source_manifest.json` records primary URLs and hashes of the three saved original PDFs. No plotted pixels were digitized, and no 3D cube or all-sky map was bulk-downloaded. The 22 MHz values are **selected-spur, local-background-subtracted radio total-intensity excesses**, not absolute full-sky temperatures. Polarization distances and dust surfaces constrain different physical components.

### NPS distance and spectrum caution

The Loop I high-latitude polarimetric association at ~100–135 pc is one interpretation of nearby material. X-ray absorption/dust studies at lower latitude put X-ray NPS material farther away: the [2020 MNRAS Gaia study](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/498/4/5863/5902411) reports approximate foreground-column saturation around 140 pc in its high-latitude selection and around 700 pc near b≈5–11°, while [Lallement et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7430517/) find the southern X-ray NPS beyond ~300 pc. [2024 eROSITA-bubble X-ray morphology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04264) argues an ~1 kpc lower bound for the X-ray NPS based on shadow association; this does not automatically re-distance the high-latitude *radio-polarized* ridge. These are tracer/latitude-dependent bounds, not mutually exchangeable measurements of one shell center. Their detailed sightline tables were not acquired here, so they are **context, not plot-ready points**. [Iwashita et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10007) fit a broadband NPS spectral break around **1 GHz**; it is a different physical spectral feature from the model's 30 MHz shell break. Its frequency-resolved region table was not acquired. [Cong et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00431) use a much larger Loop I shell in a *model*, which illustrates geometric model sensitivity but is not a new observation. A [2025 Nature Astronomy Eos-cloud result](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-025-02541-7) places a dust/H₂ cloud at 94–130 pc near Loop I; proximity is suggestive but does not establish that the cloud and radio shell coincide.

Make one four-panel loop diagnostic: for each Borka region show the published 22 MHz spur-minus-background point with its stated uncertainty against a lowsky prediction processed through the **same cuts and background operator**, with 2° model versus 1.1°×1.7° survey resolution either matched by smoothing or clearly labeled. Plot total model and each contributing shell, but compare the **aggregate** to the observation. Overlay the Borka fitted beta only as a secondary annotation; it shares the 22 MHz point and is not a second independent datum. Make a distinct distance panel of Loop I/IV sightline constraints against model near/far shell intersections, with one-sided notation for IC. A Local Bubble map/profile should label O'Neill's wall as a reused dust prior and await independent electron observables.

The extracted paper table does not supply a machine-readable mask or exact outer-background pixels. Its longitude/latitude and temperature-contour rules permit an approximation, but a faithful published-operator comparison requires the underlying 22 MHz map, masks, and background region specification; do not claim a chi-square from a simple model sample. The source's ±500 K also does not encode model/foreground selection systematics. No robust intermediate ~30–300 MHz loop-specific multi-region spectra, Loop II/III distances, separated S1/S2 total-intensity measurements, or independent Local Bubble electron-wall table were secured. Thus the four Borka points are the strongest near-term numerical intensity checks, and no measured Loop I–IV 30 MHz turnover frequency is established here. Date-bounded web searches for 2025–2026 NPS distance, Local Bubble 3D dust/thermal-X-ray geometry and Loop-specific spectra were checked on 2026-10-13; they found the 2025 Eos cloud and the eROSITA hot-gas/X-ray perspective but no newer same-operator radio measurement or compact mask/table ready for this comparison. This is a bounded finding, not a claim that none exists.
