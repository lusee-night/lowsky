# Cas A calibration: independent bounded review (2026-10-13)

- **Peak fit algebra checks out conditionally.** For the implemented source law, `S(nu)=S50*(nu/50)^alpha*exp[-tau10*((nu/10)^-2.1-5^-2.1)-tau_MW,50*(nu/50)^-2.1]` below 50 MHz. Thus `d ln S/d ln nu=alpha+2.1[tau10*(nu/10)^-2.1+tau_MW,50*(nu/50)^-2.1]`. The stored parameters give internal optical depth 0.36238 and foreground depth 0.004287 at 21.5 MHz, zero log slope, and 29,880 Jy. The two fitted peak summaries are correlated and do not supply an independent goodness-of-fit test.
- **Foreground is conditional on an old artifact.** `fit.json` infers `tau_MW,50=0.00072854` from a saved source `a00`, old S50, and assumed source formula, with `source_revision_verified=false`. In production, `ateam_integrated_k_sr` instead recomputes EM to the Cas A distance (3.4 kpc) at its HEALPix pixel and applies `tau_MW=3.28e-7*(Te/1e4)^-1.35*(nu_GHz)^-2.1*EM`. Recheck the fitted peak after that actual EM computation; this review did not establish that the old artifact and current geometry/tuning match. Whole-source exponential attenuation also omits front/back partial covering.
- **Units are consistent in the inspected code.** `S50` and modeled flux are Jy; frequency enters free-free opacity in GHz and Rayleigh–Jeans conversion in Hz; `1e-26*c²/(2*k*nu_Hz²)` converts Jy to integrated K sr. The comparison script's full-sky pixel integration applies the inverse conversion. The `S50` parameter is intrinsic at 50 MHz before foreground attenuation (modeled 50-MHz flux is about 0.073% lower), so the observational meaning of any literature S50 anchor needs care.
- **Epoch and plots need explicit provenance.** The target is the 2023.5 fitted peak; ATEAM has no secular fading, so its curve cannot be scored against the 2019 peak as the same-epoch prediction. The existing `examples/compare_bright_sources.py` plots only the saved September 2 `ateam_components.npz`, while its comparison JSON was generated October 13; changing ATEAM alone does not change that saved curve. A before/after figure should label the old saved artifact versus a regenerated or analytically evaluated new model, give both parameter sets and artifact hashes, and state that the after curve was calibrated to the 2023.5 peak. It should not present that peak as holdout validation. The existing plot correctly labels the observations as fit-derived summaries and warns about epoch/beam mismatch, but it is not a before/after calibration plot.

**Unavailable during this audit:** `examples/calibrate_cas_a.py` and any files beyond `fit.json` in `analysis/constraints-20261013/calibration/cas_a/` were absent when inspected. No simulation or plotting was run.
