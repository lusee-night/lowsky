# Conditional Cas A turnover calibration

The existing full-source power-law plus exponential-absorption term peaked near 10 MHz at about 65 kJy. [NenuFAR/URAN-2 observations at epoch 2023.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09764) report a *fitted* integrated peak of 29,880 ± 2,980 Jy at 21.5 ± 0.2 MHz. We use those two correlated summaries from one observed spectrum to determine exactly two Cas A source parameters, holding its spectral index (−0.77) and modeled Milky Way attenuation fixed. This is calibration to that observation, not a holdout test.

The calibrated `ATEAM["CAS_A"]` uses `S50=21,166.4627 Jy` and `tau10=1.808358` in place of `27,104 Jy` and `0.35`. For the unchanged 1–50 MHz source law, its log-slope at 21.5 MHz is zero and its predicted flux there is 29,880 Jy. The foreground optical depth at 50 MHz, 0.00072854, was inferred from the **canonical source harmonic's** monopole (`a00`) in the saved `outputs_our_sky_gum_gaussian_final/counterfactual_ulsa_harmonic.npz`; that artifact's exact generating source revision is unverified. `fit.json` records the artifact hash, equations and caveats. A changed foreground EM field or source formula requires recalibration. This is a simple phenomenological turnover, not a resolved front/back-shell transfer model.

Run `.venv/bin/python analysis/constraints-20261013/calibration/cas_a/plot_fit.py` from the repository root to regenerate [before/after turnover](before_after_turnover.png) and [LWA1 ratio tradeoff](lwa1_ratio_tradeoff.png) figures and `fit_comparison.json`. The 2019 fitted Cas A peak is an epoch-mismatched contextual point. LWA1 35–50 MHz Cas/Cyg ratios were **not fitted**; the predicted ratio moves from about 1.29 toward 0.96 at 35 MHz and from about 1.27 toward 0.99 at nominal 50 MHz. The observed central ratios are 1.29 ± 0.57 and 1.13 ± 0.24. The change improves the turnover target but lowers the 35–50 MHz ratio curve; shared Cyg A calibration, Cas A evolution and survey background/beam treatment prevent formal residual inference from this simple overlay. Observations above 50 MHz are shown only as context.

The 50-MHz published A-team number was an imposed external calibration-model anchor, not independent LOFAR integrated photometry. It is therefore not used as a holdout against the updated `S50` parameter. The adjusted `S50` is the source law's pre-foreground normalization at 50 MHz for the chosen 2023.5 epoch; this model has no secular fading law. It must not be represented as an all-epoch absolute Cas A value.
